Synchronization and backups

Synchronization can create or modify notes and files and propagate changes between connected services. Conflicts, network failures, and service changes can cause unexpected results, duplicates, or incomplete synchronization. Maintain independent backups of important information.

KeepSync is provided as available, without guarantees of uninterrupted operation, synchronization accuracy, or future compatibility. Connected services have their own terms and policies. Nothing here limits rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Stopping use

Disable scheduled tasks and revoke connected-service credentials to stop synchronization. Existing notes and exported files remain until you delete them from the relevant services.
