Privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026

KeepSync is a personal note synchronization application operated by Kyle. Contact: kyle4068@gmail.com.

Information processed

KeepSync processes selected Google Keep note titles, text, checklist contents, labels, timestamps, identifiers, and attachment links. It processes Markdown content and metadata in configured Google Drive folders and writes exported notes to a configured GitHub repository. Credentials and authorization tokens are used to access these services.

Purpose and Google Drive access

Information is used to export notes, synchronize changes, and maintain relationships between corresponding notes and files. The current Google Drive authorization scope permits broad Drive access; the application’s synchronization logic targets configured folders.

Storage and service providers

Synchronization runs on Google Cloud. Copies of note content are stored in the designated Google Drive folders and GitHub repository and may be downloaded to devices using Obsidian. Access to these copies follows the sharing settings of each destination. A public GitHub repository can make exported content publicly accessible.

Credentials are stored in the application’s Google Cloud configuration and Secret Manager. Google Cloud may retain operational logs containing execution and error details.

Use and disclosure

KeepSync does not sell personal information, use note content for advertising, or use it to train generalized artificial intelligence models. Data is transmitted to connected services as needed to perform the configured synchronization.

KeepSync’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Notes and exported files remain in their respective services until deleted there. Revoking access or stopping synchronization does not remove existing copies. Delete copies from Keep, Drive, GitHub, and your devices as applicable; Git history may retain earlier versions. Operational logs follow the configured Google Cloud retention settings.

Your controls

You can revoke authorization through your Google Account connections, revoke GitHub credentials, and disable scheduled synchronization. For questions or help deleting application-held information, contact the operator.

This website

These informational pages have no application login, analytics scripts, advertising, or tracking cookies added by KeepSync. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process connection information under its own privacy policy.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated effective date.
